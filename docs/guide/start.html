If you want the original game as authored, take the translation. If you have finished the League and want more to do, take the River Edition. In-game saves move freely between them and between versions.

Patching

  1. Get your own clean Pokémon FireRed (USA) v1.0 ROM. CRC32 must be DD88761C (the common “Squirrels” #1636 dump).
  2. Download the .bps from the project's GitHub Releases and apply it with a BPS patcher (Flips, Rom Patcher JS and similar).
  3. Play in mGBA (the reference emulator) or another accurate emulator. gpSP is not supported: its Cheat Device menu is cut off (issue #161).
Warning

The patch applies to the clean FireRed ROM only. It will not apply to the Chinese hack, and a River patch will not apply on top of a beta.

Saves and save states

In-game saves carry over between every beta and River version. Save states do not. Use the in-game save before you update.

This site distributes no ROMs and links to none. The patch is free and stays free.

With default bindings, SELECT + R is Backspace + S. Release L first; L + R + SELECT is the Patrol menu.

The Start menu

Pokédex, Pokémon, Bag, Pokégear, Save, Task, Trainer Card, Option, Pass Time, Help Tips. The ring does not wrap around. Help Tips explains the hack's own systems and is worth one read.

Starting a new game

  1. Press A through the naming screen (Start confirms), the disclaimer pages and Settings. B closes Settings.
  2. Go down the bedroom stairs, talk to Mom downstairs, and leave through the door mat into New Bark Town.
  3. Lyra's scene is in her house. Walk in and step left of the door to start it.
  4. Elm's lab is next. Walking up from the door starts his scene; afterwards stand at the table, face up and press A to choose a starter. All three starters work.
How verified this is

These steps come from the author's new-game test (2026-10-12, then-current build).

Reporting problems

Use the project's GitHub issues. Put your version in the title (for example “River Edition v0.5.9”), name your emulator or device, say where it happened and what you pressed, and attach a screenshot. For crashes, attach your in-game .sav from just before. Dialogue proofreading is still open (issue #24).
