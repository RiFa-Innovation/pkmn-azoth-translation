Search by item, TM move or place name. Select an item for every source the ROM shows: item balls, hidden items with their tile, gifts and quest rewards, shop stock, Frontier BP prices, River drops, and the wild Pokémon and trainers that hold it.

How complete this is

Sources are read from the game's scripts, shop lists, hidden-item events and held-item tables. About three in four items have at least one source. A location appears only when it could be pinned to one or two maps; otherwise the page shows the text the NPC says so you can recognise the scene. Roughly 210 items have no source at all: they are mostly Z-Crystals, per-species Mega Stones, Plates, Memories, Drives and engine leftovers that nothing in the game gives out. Items handed out by native code, or by scripts that choose the item at run time through a variable the scan cannot follow, may be missing.
