Search by item, TM move or place name. Select an item for every source the ROM shows: item balls, hidden items with their tile, gifts and quest rewards, shop stock, Frontier BP prices, River drops, and the wild Pokémon and trainers that hold it.

How complete this is

Sources are read from the game's scripts, shop lists, hidden-item events and held-item tables. Each gift or reward is described in plain words (for example “Goldenrod lottery: 2 digits matched” or “Gym leader's reward after you win the Storm Badge”) with the NPC's own lines underneath. The descriptions are written from those lines, so a few are general where the scene does not say more. Places are the game's area names; indoor maps that carry a leftover vanilla town name are shown as “Building” with a map id. About three in four items have a source. Roughly 210 have none: they are mostly Z-Crystals, per-species Mega Stones, Plates, Memories, Drives and engine leftovers that nothing in the game gives out. Items given by native code, or chosen at run time through a variable the scan cannot follow, may be missing.
