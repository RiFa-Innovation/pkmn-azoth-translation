Spoilers

This page names every major beat of the story.

How verified this is

Teams, levels, moves and held items on this page are read directly from the patched ROM, so they are exact. The order of events comes from the project's story notes, which came from the game's dialogue and map data and were not replayed start to finish. Where a step needs coordinates or a puzzle solution, it says so instead of guessing.

Story in order

  1. 1

    New Bark Town and Cherrygrove

    Start

    Mom, Elm's lab and your starter. A Pichu joins you, you get the Pokégear, and you meet Lyra and Yellow.

  2. 2

    Violet City and Sprout Tower

    Gym 1 · Falkner

    Dawn from Sinnoh (with Piplup) appears. The tower's “elder” is Petrel in disguise. Sprout Tower's top floor has an escort scene in a one-tile corridor; if a partner Pokémon blocks it, the game has a repair script for saves from older betas.

  3. 3

    Azalea Town and Ilex Forest

    Gym · Bugsy

    Slowpoke Well, and the first research on Mega Evolution.

  4. 4

    Goldenrod City

    Gym · Whitney

    Team Rocket (Archer, Petrel, Ariana) takes the Radio Tower to call Giovanni back.

  5. 5

    Ecruteak City

    Gym · Morty

    The Kimono Girls, the Summer Festival and the Burned Tower.

  6. 6

    Olivine, Cianwood and Mahogany

    Gyms · Jasmine, Chuck, Pryce

    The Glitter Lighthouse rescue. A Rocket device forces wild Pokémon to Mega Evolve (the “Runaway Mega Evolution” quests). Lance investigates the red Gyarados at the Lake of Rage.

  7. 7

    Blackthorn City and Dragon's Den

    Gym · Clair

    Rocket steals the Rainbow Stone shards.

  8. 8

    Whirl Islands

    Story

    Archer wakes Lugia with a Shadow-Pokémon device from Orre.

  9. 9

    Climax and the Indigo Plateau

    Story, then the League

    Sange, the “Holy Child”, plays the Time Flute and swaps places with Celebi, ending up about 150 years in the past. The Clear Bell and Rainbow Wing call Ho-Oh. After the Elite Four you meet Champion Lance.

After the credits a card reads Pokémon Azoth – Second Chapter: Rainbow. Elm gives you a Master Ball, the three Johto starters, Mega Stones and the National Dex and points you east through Route 27 and Route 26 to the Plateau. Mom later tells you that a man called Ashida left you a gift, the Mystic Ticket. The translation ends there; the River Edition continues.

Strategy

Fight them in that level order unless the story sends you somewhere else first. Falkner's whole team is at Lv 7 to 9. Morty's six peak at Lv 52, the highest of the eight.

Rematch and later teams

Several leaders also appear with stronger teams in later story scenes. Falkner (390, 527), Bugsy (391, 526), Whitney (392), Pryce (395) and Jasmine (525) each have a second team in the ROM.

Kanto leaders

Rival fights

Silver, your rival, keeps the same core of Golbat, Gengar, Weavile and Magneton/Magnezone (plus Alakazam later) and ends each team with a Johto starter. The ROM has three versions of each fight, one per starter in the last slot; they are listed together.

A second rival (his name shows as “???”) fights in the Kanto part. Each fight has a version per starter.

Team Rocket

The Pokémon League

Four members and the Champion. Team levels climb from Lv 53 (Will) to Lv 60 (Karen's Umbreon); Champion Lance's last Pokémon is Lv 64.

Strategy

Will leads with Psychic types (Xatu, Bronzong, Jynx, Exeggutor, Farigiraf, Slowbro). Koga is Poison and Bug. Bruno brings Fighting and a Steelix. Karen is Dark with Vileplume and Houndoom. Lance has three Dragonite, so bring Ice or Fairy moves.
